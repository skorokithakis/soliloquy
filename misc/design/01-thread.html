jules3h·12 points·{{k.c1.note}}

We tried it last week on a copy of the reporting box (NVMe, 64 GB RAM). Cold cache, same rollup query:

io_method   time     read MB/s
sync        6m 41s   102
worker      4m 02s   169
io_uring    3m 18s   207

Warm cache is flat across all three. So the gain is real, but only when you hit disk.
